# Contributing to Fold

Fold is early and built in the open. Bug reports, ideas, and pull requests are all welcome.

## The most useful thing right now
Run Fold on your own vault and open an issue about anything that felt confusing, fragile, or
wrong. Real first-run friction is the most valuable feedback there is.

## Filing an issue
Pick a template: a **bug** (what you ran, what happened, what you expected) or an **idea /
feedback** (what you wish it did, or where the flow or voice felt off).

## Pull requests
- One concern per PR, kept focused.
- The seven skills live in `template/.claude/commands/`, and the always-loaded doctrine in
  `template/CLAUDE.md`. Read `docs/SPEC.md` first to see how the pieces fit together.
- Match the surrounding voice: plain, warm, and every coined term glossed in one line.

## Safety (non-negotiable)
Never commit personal data, secrets, or a real vault's contents. Fold's whole point is that your
data stays yours and local; the repo must model that.
